PULSAR CONTRIBUTION

Typical and final error move more often than the shared peak.

The full hybrid is compared with IMU + optical for 160 matched scenario/profile/seed pairs. Negative deltas mean the full hybrid produced lower error.

Interpretation

Worst error remains equal in 116/160 comparisons because a common transient fault peak can dominate both suites. That does not imply that processed pulsar timing had no contribution to the rest of the window.
